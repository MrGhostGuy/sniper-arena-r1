# R1 Sharpshooter

An original arena sniping game built for the Rabbit R1 (240×282), by **Jeff H. (MrGhost)**.
All art, item names and code are original to this creation.

**Version 6.1** — callsign entry fixed (sturdier name box + a built-in letter picker on the title screen, tap ABC).
**Version 6.0** — pregame lobby with map voting, 2 new maps (Neon Spire, Back
Alley), TDM removed, FFA is a race to 30 kills, FLAG HUNT ranked by flag points,
a saved player callsign, and a fanfare winner screen.
(5.0 rebuilt the rarity ladder: LEGENDARY on top, ULTRA below it, unique tracers.)

## The Match (3:00, respawns)
- **Set your callsign** in the name box on the title screen — it is saved, shown
  on the scoreboard, and announced if you win.
- **PREGAME LOBBY** — before every match you see all 6 players and their loadouts,
  and vote between 2 maps. Bots vote too; most votes wins the map.
- **FREE-FOR-ALL** — every sniper for themselves. First to **30 kills** wins
  instantly, or the most kills when the buzzer hits.
- **FLAG HUNT** — one flag; kill the carrier to take it. Only the carrier scores
  flag points (+10/s). Winner = most **flag points**; everyone's kills are still
  shown on the final board.
- **Winner screen** — every match ends with the winner's name announced with
  fanfare, confetti and rotating rays before the results board.
- **Death screen** shows who killed you, the sniper they used, and the live
  scoreboard — then hit RESPAWN. Your best run of the match replays as the
  **BEST PLAY** at the end.
- **Medals** pop for kill chains (DOUBLE KILL, TRIPLE KILL, MULTI-KILL…) and
  streaks: 5 BLOODTHIRSTY · 10 MERCILESS · 15 RELENTLESS · 20 BRUTAL · 25 NUCLEAR.
- **5 maps**, voted in the lobby: Downtown Rooftops, Neon Docks, Dust Canyon,
  **Neon Spire** (futuristic neon towers, holo-billboards, hover pods) and
  **Back Alley** (brick walls, fire escapes — enemies hide behind and *inside*
  dumpsters and oil-barrel clusters).

## Combat
- **Damage model**: headshots always kill. Body shots deal your rifle's DMG
  against 100 HP — some rifles need two hits. Enemy HP bars show the damage.
- **Bolt-action** rifles cycle between shots; two **semi-autos** rip fast.
  Every rifle has its own damage, mag size, rechamber and ADS speed (in the Armory).
- **Rarity ladder**: Common < Rare < Epic < Mythic < Ultra < **Legendary** (top).
  Stats follow the tier — Legendaries are the fastest, steadiest rifles in the game.
- **Legendary + Ultra rifles fire special projectiles**, each with a listed bonus,
  a themed ADS reticle and a matching shot sound:
  - **Suncrown Regent — SOLAR LANCE**: a beam that pierces EVERY enemy in line
  - **Inferno Parade — IMMOLATION**: flaming rounds, heavy burn, fiery explosions
  - **Aurora Veil — CHAIN LIGHTNING**: Zeus-pylon rifle; arcs to 2 enemies (50% / 25%)
  - **Kraken Depth — DEEP FREEZE**: chills enemies (aim slowed 55%), kills shatter
  - **Widow Waltz — EVENT HORIZON**: kills implode for 65 dmg nearby
  - **Monarch Decree — GILDED ROUNDS**: kills pay +100% coins
  - **GHOST LEGACY — SPECTRE**: phase rounds that hit enemies through cover
  - **Phantom Zero — ZERO POINT**: rail spike, +25% dmg — body shots kill
  - **Last Light — LAST LIGHT**: flare rounds that blind nearby enemies, resetting their aim
- **Epic + Mythic rifles fire unique tracers** (Pulse, Ember, Frost, Arc, Void),
  named on their Armory stat line.
- **Cases**: Standard (100⬢), Elite (300⬢), Ultra (800⬢) drop rifles or one of
  **12 kill effects**; the **Kill FX Chest** (250⬢) drops kill effects only.
  Every player starts with **one chest of each kind**. Opening a case plays a
  spinning prize reel that slows onto your drop, with a rarity-tiered reveal.
  Duplicates convert back into coins.
- **Pause menu** (top-right): aim sensitivity, restart match, exit.

## Controls
- Drag to look · scroll wheel pans · ZOOM button or `Z` cycles scope
- FIRE button, tap the scope, side button, or `Space` to shoot
- Hold the big DUCK button (or `↓`) to take cover — their shots miss
